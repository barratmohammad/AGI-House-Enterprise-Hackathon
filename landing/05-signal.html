Method

Stitch. Price. Draft.

Nothing here runs on a guess, and every number on the page decomposes.

01

Reports become threads

Same ticket number, same email chain, same alert refiring — merged automatically. When two reports are merely likely to be the same fault, a judge proposes the match and a person accepts or rejects it.

02

Threads get a price

A live day counts 1 at low severity and 8 at critical; resolved days cost nothing. Each person pulled in widens it. A fix that failed and came back multiplies it. Alerts aren't effort — only what a person wrote.

03

Cost becomes a case

The thread already holds the input, the wrong answer, and — from whoever verified the fix — the right one. The model drafts the case; every quote it cites is checked against the event it came from.
