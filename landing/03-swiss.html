Method

Three moves, in order.

01

Stitch

Same ticket number, same email chain, same alert refiring — merged automatically. Merely similar? A judge proposes, a person decides. Nothing joins on a guess.

02

Price

How bad, for how long. How many people it pulled in. Whether the fix failed and it came back. What a human had to write. Resolved days cost nothing.

03

Draft

The thread already holds the input, the wrong answer, and the right one. The model writes the case; every quote it cites is checked against its source event.
