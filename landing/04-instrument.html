Method

Three instruments, in series

01 — STITCH

Joined on certainty

Same ticket number, same email chain, same alert refiring — merged automatically. Merely similar gets proposed by a judge and confirmed by a person. Nothing merges on a guess.

02 — PRICE

Exposure × reach × recurrence + effort

A live day counts 1 at low severity and 8 at critical. Resolved days cost nothing. Every person pulled in widens it; a fix that failed and came back multiplies it.

03 — DRAFT

Grounded, then checked

The thread already holds the input, the wrong answer and the right one. The model drafts the case; every quote it cites is looked up in the event it claims to come from.

Limits

Some readings are not eval cases

Thread 8 is a run of latency alerts — a real incident with a real cost, but not a test of anything the assistant said. Throughline drafts a threshold check and states the limitation instead of inventing a prompt. An instrument that never reads zero is broken.
